01 — About

Planning for people, land, and the space in between.

I'm a Bachelor of Urban & Regional Planning from Institut Teknologi Sumatera, with hands-on experience across spatial, environmental, and social planning. My work spans Regional Spatial Plans (RTRW), Environmental Protection & Management Plans (RPPLH), coastal-area development, and provincial studies on extreme poverty.

Selected projects

Thesis

Industrial–Settlement Spatial Pattern & Association

Cikarang, Bekasi · 2024

Detected industrial and residential building footprints from Google Open Building, then ran kernel-density and nearest-neighbour distance analysis to find where the two land uses concentrate — and collide.

  • Google Earth Engine
  • Python
  • IBM SPSS

Poverty Characteristic Mapping — East Kalimantan

Bontang City · 2023

Prepared and updated P3KE target data for extremely poor families, mapping distribution hotspots and profiling each sub-district by employment, education, housing, sanitation, and stunting risk.

  • ArcGIS
  • P3KE Data

RPPLH — South Papua Province

Environmental Protection & Management Plan · 2024

Inventoried land-ecosystem vulnerability province-wide and mapped sacred and customary lands affected by the Food Estate program — flagging social-conflict risk before development proceeds.

  • Excel

Jabung & Coastal Area Development Plan

East Lampung Regency · 2024

Applied macro–micro gravity analysis to set a thematic base for agropolitan and minapolitan coastal zones, weighing policy direction, demographics, and each area's regional potential.

  • IBM SPSS
  • ArcGIS
